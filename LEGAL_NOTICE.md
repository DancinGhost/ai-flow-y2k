## 1. Project Overview
`ai-flow-y2k` is an independent, open-source development tool designed to intercept, monitor, and approve AI agent tool executions and stream reasoning traces in a local environment.

## 2. Descriptive Aesthetic Themes
The visual styles provided in this application are creative tributes to popular retro digital art movements:
- **Vaporwave**: Inspired by 1980s and 1990s synthwave, neon grids, and pastel gradients.
- **Digital Rain**: Inspired by early terminal green phosphor displays and digital streams.
- **Aero Gloss**: Inspired by early 2000s skeuomorphic interfaces, translucent glass, and gloss highlights.

All themes, color palettes, gradients, and styling routines in this project are procedurally coded. No proprietary textures, fonts, or assets are distributed with this software.
