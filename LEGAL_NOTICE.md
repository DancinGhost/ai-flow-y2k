## 1. Project Overview
`ai-flow-y2k` is an independent, open-source development tool designed to intercept, monitor, and inspect AI agent tool executions and stream reasoning traces in a local environment.

## 2. Descriptive Aesthetic Themes
The visual styles provided in this application are creative tributes to popular retro digital art movements:
- **Vaporwave**: Inspired by 1980s and 1990s synthwave, neon grids, and pastel gradients.
- **Digital Rain**: Inspired by early terminal green phosphor displays and digital streams.
- **Frutiger Aero**: Inspired by early 2000s skeuomorphic interfaces, translucent glass, and gloss highlights.

All themes, color palettes, gradients, and styling routines in this project are procedurally rendered in C++ and Qt6 without using or bundling proprietary textures, commercial fonts, or copyrighted franchise assets.

## 4. Privacy & Personally Identifiable Information (PII)
`ai-flow-y2k` operates strictly as a local reverse proxy (`http://127.0.0.1:8765`). It contains no telemetry, tracking, or remote phone-home capabilities. No personal data, credentials, tokens, or agent payloads are transmitted to any third party.
